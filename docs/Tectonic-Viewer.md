# Experimental tectonic terrain viewer

**Current v7 preset (2026-09-18):** default horizontal plate spacing is 2,048 blocks, four times smaller in X/Z than the immediately prior 8,192-block v5/v6 preset. Vertical relief, the 256-block world height and sea Y63 are unchanged. The v6 above-sea curve remains: normalized tectonic rise `t = rise / (landHeight + plateRelief + plateTilt)` occupies `t / (t + 0.25)` of the available vertical range. Saved v5/v6 URLs divide horizontal location, sampling step and plate spacing by four exactly once; compatible vertical settings remain. This is a versioned world-scale change, not bit-identical resampling of old chunks.

Hover over a completed map for exact sampled X/Z, eroded/original surface, erosion, climate, wind, rock, soil/rockhead, drainage and water details. Coordinates appear immediately; details are debounced 160 ms and cached for the latest 256 points. Moving away, dragging or changing the view invalidates pending results. The tooltip also works inside fullscreen. Click still opens the full inspector.

Open [the local tectonic laboratory](http://localhost:8787/tectonics.html). Start the server with `./genesis.ps1 serve` or `./genesis.sh serve` if needed. Existing `/` and `/continents.html` pages retain their original models.

This is the separate Java 21 research candidate `tectonic-experimental`: terrain `tectonic-terrain-v7`, climate `terrain-climate-v1`, wind `wind-field-v1`, substrate `terrain-substrate-v1`, hydrology `continental-hydrology-v4`, and fluvial interpretation `fluvial-network-v2`. Its [Minecraft-native scale contract](../research/minecraft-native-tectonic-terrain.md) makes size 1 a 256-block column with sea Y63. Raw terrain scales exactly for sizes 2–4; climate, erosion and routing run on bounded complete-family supports. [Climate/ground drainage](../research/climate-ground-hydrology.md), [continental hydraulic erosion](../research/continental-erosion.md) and [the fluvial revamp](../research/fluvial-hydrology-revamp.md) define the ordering and limitations. The Minecraft counterpart is `genesis-columns-v5`.

## Controls and configuration

All 46 fields are in the top bar. The expandable panel contains 21 world parameters, including `size`, precipitation scale and erosion strength. **Eroded terrain** is the initial view. **Before erosion**, **Erosion depth**, and **Terrain hardness** expose the substrate and erosion inputs/results. Erosion exposure defaults to 700; zero disables hydraulic incision. Substrate resistance is material-derived in the coupled path, and synthetic plate age controls exposure.

- **Wind direction** and **Wind speed** show the analytic stream-function field. It is globally continuous, divergence-free and bounded away from zero; it does not start or terminate at continent or viewport borders.
- **Humidity** and **Rainfall** show the 24-pass bounded moisture solve. Maritime water replenishes humidity, upwind advection crosses pre-incision relief, and positive directional uplift creates windward enhancement plus lee-side rain shadows. The rainfall control is a model annual scale, not a weather event or seasonal forecast.
- **Rock type**, **Soil depth**, **Bedrock elevation/depth**, **Infiltration**, **Runoff fraction** and **Ground drainage** expose the five-class substrate and annual partition. Runoff + infiltration + evapotranspiration equals 1,000 permille at each land node; only surface runoff enters the river ledger. “Bedrock” here means coherent parent rock, not Minecraft's unbreakable bottom block.
- **Rivers + lakes** and its diagnostics use the complete irregular continental-family footprint and final post-erosion coarse bed, never the canvas boundary. Physical current threads and bankfull corridors use the shared fluvial query. Lake components are face-connected and level; incoming river stage blends to the receiving lake surface. When a physical current is narrower than one display pixel, a bounded pixel-footprint cue keeps it legible; the inspector's `insideCurrent` remains the physical mask. Pink is unresolved/unavailable support.
- **Height + contours** shows the original surface before erosion with fixed colors and display-only isolines. Spacing accepts 0 (off) or 1–256 actual blocks. Every fifth line is heavier. Intervals coarsen deterministically at wide zooms.
- **Size** scales the world, not the PNG. World height is `256 * size`, sea level is `63 * size`, and effective plate spacing is `base plate spacing * size`. The default base spacing is 2,048 blocks.

Use **Apply world** to commit edited inputs. Draft controls never silently alter the displayed/exported frame. The wide-area land target remains about 30%, not a per-viewport quota. Continental families contain 1–4 plates and are usually singletons.

Drag to pan continuously, scroll or use +/- to zoom, and click a completed map to inspect it. Cached terrain follows the pointer immediately, even while other tiles load. A brief pause during dragging or releasing the pointer requests missing tiles. The normal raster is 384 square. **Fullscreen** gives the map panel the browser fullscreen surface at 512 square; exit or Escape restores 384. Overview centers on the origin using effective plate spacing.

Rendering uses fixed 128×128 world tiles, up to four simultaneous requests and a 128-tile browser LRU (about 8 MiB of decoded RGBA pixels, plus browser overhead). Cache keys include every terrain, climate, wind, substrate, hydrology and fluvial version plus world/layer/scale/phase configuration. Returning to a cached region makes no generator requests; equal in-flight tiles share a promise. Display tiles never become drainage or climate boundaries.

Tiles appear progressively. Exports assemble only a completed viewport into a PNG snapshot with matching coordinates and settings. The land percentage describes the loaded covering tiles, including offscreen margins. Independent stitching tests verify coupled physical layers and contour halos against a single raster. A display-only sub-pixel river cue may vary with zoom; committed world fields do not.

The URL stores all six model versions, seed, coordinates, step, field, all 21 parameters and contour spacing. v5/v6 links migrate horizontal values by four; incompatible water/climate versions require reopening the current preset. Export downloads the completed PNG plus matching JSON. IDs, water ledgers and exported bedrock volume remain exact decimal strings.

## HTTP and state contracts

`/api/tectonic/meta`, `/api/tectonic/render` and `/api/tectonic/sample` are read-only endpoints. Unknown, duplicate or out-of-range inputs fail closed. Each response carries its full world configuration. PNG headers include terrain, hydrology, fluvial, climate, wind and substrate versions, render time, local land fraction, contour interval, native scale, world height and sea level. Point JSON includes the same dimensions and versions plus climate/wind, ground composition/partition, exact IDs, family ledgers, area/order, hydraulics and lake spill metadata. Colors use fixed scales, never crop normalization.

New renders use cancellation plus revision tokens. Late renders or inspections cannot replace a newer frame. Exports remain disabled for stale/failed frames and serialize the last completed configuration.

Hydrology preflights complete support and rejects a request before building any root above **20 families** or **4,000,000 support vertices**. The coupled retained-array estimate is about **80.5 bytes per vertex**, so the byte guard is **322,000,000 bytes** at the same vertex ceiling; temporary climate solve arrays, raster/map storage and JVM overhead are additional. The current default 384 fixture uses 16 families / 3,287,184 support vertices. Equal cold roots coalesce, at most four distinct roots build concurrently, and a hydrology context retains at most 12 roots. These are work/memory bounds, not a portable latency promise. Near the numeric guard, unavailable support remains explicit status 0 rather than a clipped outlet.

## Verification and limits

`ClimateGroundGates` checks numerical wind divergence, speed floor, continuity/signed coordinates, controlled windward-versus-lee rainfall, bounded humidity, material drainage counterfactuals, exact runoff/infiltration/ET closure, effective source conversion and unchanged root dimensions after the fourfold X/Z shrink. Terrain, hydrology, erosion and fluvial gates retain their independent topology, source-walk, conservation, lake/backwater, curve and concurrency checks. `TectonicViewGates` traverses all 46 layers and verifies coupled metadata/inspection, crop/zoom behavior and work guards; tile gates prove pixel stitching. `node harness/gates/tectonic-viewer-contracts.cjs [baseURL]` checks the 21 controls, versioned state, navigation, inspection, fullscreen, stale responses and exports. Generated maps provide image QA; these tests do not claim real GTNH fluid behavior.

The output remains a 2.5D integer surface. It has no caves/arches, temperature, seasons, snowpack, transient atmosphere, groundwater/baseflow, karst conduits, lake storage/hypsometry, fluvial sediment transport, bars/deltas or certified fine-grid channel descent. Erosion and proxy mass wasting are bounded static generation stages, not elapsed-time simulations. Generated Minecraft water is stationary vanilla metadata-0 water: computed velocity is diagnostic and exerts no modeled force. Lake shorelines still require an in-game fluid-update soak test. Size greater than 1 intentionally repeats a canonical value over an N-by-N footprint and emits heights in multiples of N; standard Minecraft 1.7.10 integration should use size 1 unless a tall-world stack is explicitly supported.
