Read this map correctly

Blue water touches this raster’s boundary. Purple water is enclosed in the sampled D8 grid. Neither label proves real-world connectivity between samples or beyond the window.

Connected-water mode ends routes when they enter blue water and closes dry edges. If no connected water exists, routing is unresolved: pink, not fabricated outlets. Open-edge mode retains the original artificial-edge experiment.

Fill depth is the minimum rise needed to reach a declared outlet. It is not a simulated lake level. Routes follow a deterministic flood tree, including across flats; they are not steepest-slope rivers.

Runoff is contributing land-cell count, not rainfall or discharge in physical units. Thin cyan marks show resolved land cells meeting the threshold. Click a cell to trace its route to a terminal. Subregion audits use the same complete graph; they do not reroute a cropped map.
