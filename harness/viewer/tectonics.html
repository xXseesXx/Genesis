Reading the layers

Terrain = crustal base (including global offset) + small detail + positive and negative boundary relief.

Compression raises mean plate height and tilts the compressed side upward; extension does the reverse. This is a bounded kinematic approximation, not a geological simulation.

Mountain joins are allowed within a continental family. Inter-family ocean straits still enforce the four-plate continent budget.

Regimes: gray quiet · brown collision · purple subduction · teal continental rift · blue spreading · green mixed rift · gold transform.

Plate IDs and seeds stay exact signed 64-bit integers. World heights do not depend on crop or zoom; contour stroke rendering does depend on zoom.
