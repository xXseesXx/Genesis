Reading the layers

Terrain composes native-block crust, detail and boundary relief, then smoothly fits that signal into the finite build column.

Continuous divergence-free wind carries maritime moisture across relief. Orographic lift increases windward rain while moisture loss creates lee-side rain shadows.

Rock type controls substrate resistance and permeability; climate and slope set soil depth and the runoff/infiltration split consumed by erosion and rivers.

Compression raises mean plate height and tilts the compressed side upward; extension does the reverse. This is a bounded kinematic approximation, not a geological simulation.

Mountain joins are allowed within a continental family. Inter-family ocean straits still enforce the four-plate continent budget.

Regimes: gray quiet · brown collision · purple subduction · teal continental rift · blue spreading · green mixed rift · gold transform.
